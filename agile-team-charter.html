Reflection (10 Marks)

Our charter was living because we referred to it when conflict arose about pricing. Initially we argued R999 Jean vs R150 hoodie - charter's core value "Affordability" helped decide keep both: R999 aspirational on https://item-hive.vercel.app/ product page, R120-R240 core for NSFAS. Working agreement "Trello first" reduced friction when Olly linked 2 random cards without tasks - we used charter to enforce checklist before moving to Doing. Without operational boundaries (20-27 Oct + vercel deploy) we would have scope creep. With them, we shipped Home page to vercel.app on time. This aligns with rubric: charter reduces friction and aligns daily execution.
